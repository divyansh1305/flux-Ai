Privacy policy

Plain-English summary of how Flux handles your data.

What we store

Your name, email, and the transactions, budgets and goals you add or import. We never ask for bank passwords, card numbers or UPI PINs.

Statement uploads

Uploaded CSV files are read once to show you a preview and are not stored. Only the rows you confirm are saved.

Voice

Speech is turned into text by your browser's built-in speech service. Flux receives only the text of your command, and it is not stored apart from the transaction it creates.

AI coach

When the AI coach is enabled, Flux sends a summary of your numbers (totals, categories, budgets, goals and your first name) to Google Gemini to generate an answer. Your email and raw notes are not sent.

Automatic tracking

Flux only sees bank messages you paste or share with it, or that you choose to forward. It cannot read your inbox.

The optional Flux Companion Android app reads notifications only from payment and bank apps, and only after you give consent and turn on Notification access. Chats, OTPs, offers and reminders are filtered out on your phone and never sent. A matching payment notification is sent over HTTPS to your account and stored as a transaction, along with up to 120 characters of the original text so you can check it. On iPhone, a Shortcuts automation you create sends only messages containing the word you choose (for example "debited") to your private Flux link. Turn it off any time in Settings → Automatic tracking.

Gmail connect (optional)

If you connect Gmail, Flux gets read-only access and searches only for bank alert emails (words like "debited" or "UPI"). It reads each matching email once to find the amount, merchant and date, then saves just that transaction. Email content is never stored, and Flux cannot send, delete or change your email. The access key is stored encrypted. Disconnect any time in Settings, or in your Google account under Security → Third-party connections.

Your controls

Download all your data or permanently delete your account any time from Settings.

Not financial advice

Flux helps you budget and save. It does not give investment, tax or legal advice.
