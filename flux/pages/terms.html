Terms of use

Last updated 6 November 2026. Plain language on purpose.

In short

Kitna is a free budgeting tool. It shows you what you've spent and how much is safe to spend today. It never moves your money, never offers loans and never sells your data. Use it as a helper, and check important decisions yourself.

Who can use Kitna

You must be 18 or older and use Kitna for your own money. One account per person. Keep your email account secure: anyone who can read your email can sign in with a code.

What Kitna does

Kitna reads the bank and UPI messages you choose to share (by pasting, your phone, Gmail if you connect it, or statements you upload), works out your payments, and shows you numbers like "safe to spend today". It can make mistakes: a bank message may be read wrongly or missed. You can fix any payment, and you should check large amounts against your bank.

What Kitna does not do

Kitna is not a bank, a payment app, a lender or a financial adviser. It cannot pay anyone, hold money or see your bank password or UPI PIN. Nothing in Kitna is investment, tax or legal advice. Reminders about late refunds explain the RBI rules; asking your bank for a refund is still up to you.

Friends and reminders

When you split a payment or send a reminder, Kitna only keeps the names and amounts you enter. It never contacts your friends: you choose whether to send the message. Be kind: only add people you really shared costs with.

Fair use

Don't try to break, overload or misuse Kitna, read other people's data, or use it for anything illegal. We may limit or close accounts that do.

Your data

Your data is yours. See exactly what Kitna keeps in Settings → What Kitna knows, download all of it, or delete your account at any time. Details are in the privacy policy.

Availability

Kitna is a new, free service run by a small team. We work to keep it running and your data safe, with daily backups, but we can't promise it will always be available or error-free. We may change or stop features, and will tell you in the app before anything that affects your data.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, Kitna is provided "as is" and we are not responsible for losses from decisions made using it, from errors in bank messages, or from services we don't control (your bank, Gmail, your phone).
