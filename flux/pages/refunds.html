Refunds and cancellation

Last updated 24 November 2026.

What you pay for

Kitna is free for personal use and for collecting rent from up to 5 tenants. Kitna Rent Pro costs ₹299 for 1 month or ₹2,999 for 12 months (prices include any applicable taxes) and covers up to 100 tenants.

No automatic renewal

Pro is a one-time payment for the period you choose. Nothing renews or charges you again on its own. When the period ends, your account goes back to the free plan and nothing is deleted.

Refunds

Changed your mind? Ask within 7 days of paying and we'll refund the full amount, no questions asked. After 7 days we refund the unused whole months of a 12-month plan. Write to __CONTACT__ from the email you sign in with. Refunds go back to the same card, UPI or bank account through Razorpay and usually arrive in 5–7 working days.

If a payment fails

If money left your account but Pro didn't turn on, it is either activated within a few minutes or returned by Razorpay automatically, usually within 5–7 working days. You can also write to us and we'll sort it out.

Delivery

Kitna is an online service. Pro turns on in your account as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment; there is nothing to ship.

Rent you collect

Your tenants pay you directly to your own UPI ID or bank account. That money never passes through Kitna or Razorpay, so Kitna cannot refund or reverse rent; please settle any rent dispute with your tenant directly.
