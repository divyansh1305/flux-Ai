The problem.

Paying by UPI doesn't feel like spending. Your money is spread across GPay, PhonePe, bank SMS and statements, and none of them agree on one number. Budget apps die because typing every chai is tiring.

How Flux helps.

Logs itself

Turn it on once. Every UPI and bank payment shows up in Flux by itself, sorted into food, travel, bills and more. No typing.

Matches your bank

The same payment can show up in a UPI app, an SMS and your statement. Flux counts it once, and checks itself against the balance your bank reports.

Safe to spend today

One number that lasts until your next payday: what you can spend today after bills, your plans (like Friday's dinner) and a safety cushion you choose. Tap it to see the maths.

"Is mahine food pe kitna gaya?" "Set a 4,000 food budget." Ask in English, Hindi or Hinglish, by typing or by voice.
