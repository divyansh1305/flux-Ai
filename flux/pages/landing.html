The problem.

Paying by UPI doesn't feel like spending. Most young people know they overspend but have no simple way to see it or stop it, and cashback rewards spending more.

74%

of UPI users surveyed by IIIT Delhi said UPI made them spend more.

27%

of Indian adults are financially literate (National Financial Literacy Strategy 2025 report).

5.1%

household net financial savings as a share of GDP in FY23, a multi-decade low (RBI).

How Flux fixes it.

⏸

Pause before you pay

Scan any UPI QR. Flux shows the budget impact and goal delay, then opens GPay, PhonePe or Paytm with everything filled in.

⚡

Tracks itself

Payments made through Flux log automatically. Share a bank SMS or upload a statement, and every transaction is categorised for you.

🔁

The anti-cashback

Every expense rounds up into your goal, only when you have the balance. When a goal grows, invest it in an index fund in your own Zerodha account.

🎙

Just talk to it

“How much did I spend on food this week?” “Set a 4,000 food budget.” “Rahul ko 200 bhejo.” English, Hindi and Hinglish.
