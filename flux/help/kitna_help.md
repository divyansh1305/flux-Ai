# Kitna help guide
This is everything the Kitna helper knows. It answers only from this guide.

## What Kitna is
Kitna ("how much?") tells you how much you can safely spend today, and logs your UPI and bank payments by itself. Tagline: "Pehle Kitna, phir kharcha." Kitna never moves money, never sees your UPI PIN or bank password, never offers loans, never shows ads and never sells data. It is only for people aged 18 and over.

## The tabs
Pay in GPay, PhonePe or Paytm exactly as you always do. Kitna works around the payment: it adds it by itself and keeps today's number right.
- Today [/app]: today's number, "Just paid" (new payments to sort with one tap), anything that needs you, coming up, recent payments, and "Cash payment" / "Split a bill".
- Activity [/app/transactions]: every payment, search, filter money in or money out, tap one to edit or delete it.
- Plan [/app/plans]: plans and bills coming up, and your money setup (payday and safety cushion).
- You: Auto-tracking, Help, Friends, Ask Kitna, Add bank statement, Scan a QR, Settings.

## Just paid (sorting new payments)
Payments Kitna adds by itself wait under "Just paid" on Today [/app]. Tap a category chip (Food, Groceries, Transport…) to file one, or "More" for every category. If the bank hid the name ("UPI payment ••3484"), type who it was first. Kitna remembers: the next payment to the same person or shop is named and filed by itself and won't ask again. "Looks right" keeps all of Kitna's guesses.

## Today's number (safe to spend)
Kitna takes the money you have, adds money you are sure is coming, takes away bills due, your plans, and your safety cushion, then shares what is left over the days until your next payday (or month end if your income varies). Tap "How?" on Today to see every line. Money you don't spend today is added to tomorrow. If the number shows in amber, you are spending faster than your money allows.
To change your payday or cushion: Upcoming tab → Your money setup → Edit [/app/plans].

## Setting up (first time)
1. Sign in with your email (Kitna emails you a 6-digit code, no password) or with Google.
2. Confirm you are 18 or older.
3. Answer when your money comes in (same date every month, or it varies) and roughly how much is in your bank now.
4. Connect your payments so Kitna logs them by itself (see Automatic tracking).
On iPhone, add Kitna to your Home Screen: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open Kitna from the Home Screen icon.

## Automatic tracking
Settings → Automatic tracking [/app/settings#auto].
- Android (best): install the Kitna Companion app, tap "Link the Kitna Companion app" on the same phone, then allow Notification access. It reads payment notifications from GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED, Amazon Pay, WhatsApp Pay, bank apps, and bank SMS shown by your SMS app on any phone brand. It has no SMS permission and drops OTPs and chats on the phone. Test it by paying ₹1 by UPI.
- Gmail (iPhone and Android): tap Connect Gmail. Kitna reads only bank alert emails, read-only, and adds the last 30 days at once. If it says "Reconnect Gmail", tap Reconnect.
- iPhone Shortcut: Settings → Automatic tracking → iPhone → "Set up with an iPhone Shortcut". It forwards bank SMS to your personal link. Keep the link private.
- If you block notifications for your SMS app or UPI app in phone settings, Kitna cannot see those payments. Turn them back on.
- If Kitna says "A payment may be missing", its balance doesn't match your bank's. Add the missing payment, or tap the step to match your bank.

## Scan a QR (optional)
You → Scan a QR [/app/pay]. Most people never need this: just pay in your UPI app. Use it to see what a shop payment does to today's number before paying. Kitna then opens your UPI app; pay there with your PIN (UPI apps don't accept payments started by other apps, so you scan or pay inside the UPI app). When the bank email arrives, Kitna files it with the shop's name.
- No camera? Tap "QR from photo", or type a UPI ID.
- Camera not starting on iPhone: Settings app → Safari → Camera → Allow, then reopen Kitna. Or tap "Start camera".
- Camera blocked on Android Chrome: tap the lock icon in the address bar → Permissions → Camera → Allow.
- Paid in cash? Tap "Paid in cash? Add it" or Add on Today.

## Adding or fixing a payment
- Add: Today → Add, or Activity → Add. Choose money out or money in, amount, category, date.
- Edit or delete: Activity → tap the payment.
- Wrong category: tap the payment, pick the right one and tick "always use this". Kitna fixes older ones too and learns.
- Voice: say "Spent 250 on Swiggy" or "Papa se 2000 mile" to the voice assistant (mic button, or Ctrl/⌘+K on a laptop).

## Plans and bills
Upcoming tab [/app/plans] → Add. Example: "Dinner Friday ₹2,000". Kitna keeps that money aside in today's number. Mark it done when paid. Money coming in can be marked "sure" (counted) or "maybe" (not counted).

## Money checks
Kitna warns you about: a failed payment whose money hasn't come back (it follows RBI timelines: T+1 for transfers, T+5 for merchants, and the bank owes ₹100 a day after that), being charged twice, and a subscription price going up. You can dismiss each for good.

## Friends (split and get paid back)
You → Friends [/app/friends]. Split a payment and Kitna counts only your share. When a friend pays you back by UPI, Kitna ticks them off by itself if the amount and name match; if only the amount matches, it asks you. Add your UPI ID in Settings → Language and UPI ID so "please pay me back" messages include it.

## Bank statements
You → Add bank statement [/app/import]. Upload a PDF (password-protected is fine, Kitna asks for the password) or CSV from your bank. Payments already in Kitna are not added twice.

## Ask Kitna (money questions)
You → Ask Kitna [/app/coach]: questions about your own money, like "Where did most of my money go this month?". It uses only your numbers and never recommends specific stocks, funds or loans.

## Reminders
Settings → Reminders [/app/settings#reminders]. At most 3 a day: refunds that are late, double charges, bills due tomorrow, friends who owe you, a Sunday summary, and the morning number. On iPhone, reminders need Kitna on the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or newer). Everything also shows under the bell.

## Language
Settings → Language and UPI ID: English or Hinglish for the app's screens. This helper can answer in English, Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam and Punjabi.

## Appearance
Settings → Appearance: Auto (follows your phone), Light or Dark.

## Your data, privacy and account
Settings → Your data [/app/settings#data]: see what Kitna knows about you, export everything, or delete your account. Deleting removes all your data and disconnects Gmail. Privacy policy: [/privacy]. Terms: [/terms].
Kitna stores amounts and short payment notes, never full emails, never OTPs, never chats.

## Problems and contact
- "Something's broken", ideas or complaints: You → Tell us something. The team reads every message.
- Signed out or code not arriving: check spam, wait 30 seconds, request a new code.
- Numbers look wrong: check Activity for a missing or doubled payment, then Upcoming → Your money setup.
- Kitna cannot: move money, show your bank balance live from the bank, give loans, or pay for you.

## Price
Kitna is free.
