Privacy policy

Plain-English summary of how Kitna handles your data.

Who can use Kitna

Kitna is for people aged 18 and over. You confirm this when you sign up.

What we store

Your name, email, and the payments, plans, budgets and goals you add, import or connect, plus the bank balances shown in your bank's alerts (used to check Kitna matches your bank). We never ask for bank passwords, card numbers or UPI PINs. In the app, Settings → What Kitna knows shows all of it, counted.

Statement uploads

Uploaded PDF and CSV statements are read once in memory to show you a preview and are not stored. A PDF password is used only to open that file and is never saved. Only the rows you confirm are saved.

Friends

Names and amounts you add on the Friends screen (for example "Rahul owes ₹500 for dinner") are stored in your account only, so Kitna can tick them off when the payment arrives. Kitna never contacts your friends: "Remind" opens a message on your phone that you choose to send.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, your browser gives Kitna an address to send notifications to (through Google, Apple or Mozilla's push service, depending on your phone). Each message is encrypted for your phone only, so the push service can't read it. Turn them off in Settings any time; the address is deleted then, and with your account.

Voice

Speech is turned into text by your browser's built-in speech service. Kitna receives only the text of your command, and it is not stored apart from the transaction it creates.

AI coach

When the AI coach is enabled, Kitna sends a summary of your numbers (totals, categories, budgets, goals and your first name) to Google Gemini to generate an answer. Your email and raw notes are not sent.

Automatic tracking

Kitna only sees bank messages you paste or share with it, that your phone sends to it, or that come from Gmail if you connect it (below).

The optional Kitna Companion Android app reads notifications only from payment and bank apps, and only after you give consent and turn on Notification access. Chats, OTPs, offers and reminders are filtered out on your phone and never sent. A matching payment notification is sent over HTTPS to your account and stored as a transaction, along with up to 120 characters of the original text so you can check it. On iPhone, a Shortcuts automation you create sends only messages containing the word you choose (for example "debited") to your private Kitna link. Turn it off any time in Settings → Automatic tracking.

Gmail connect (optional)

If you connect Gmail, Kitna gets read-only access and searches only for bank alert emails (words like "debited" or "UPI"). It reads each matching email once to find the amount, merchant and date, then saves just that transaction. Email content is never stored, and Kitna cannot send, delete or change your email. The access key is stored encrypted. Disconnect any time in Settings, or in your Google account under Security → Third-party connections.

How we improve Kitna

Kitna records which screens and features you use (for example "opened Plans" or "a bank message couldn't be read"), never amounts, merchant names or message text. We only ever look at totals across everyone, to see what works and what to fix. This record is deleted with your account and after 13 months at most.

What we never do

We never sell or share your data, never show ads, never offer loans, and never move money. Kitna can't pay anyone or touch your bank account.

Your controls

Download all your data or permanently delete your account any time from Settings. Deleting your account also removes Kitna's access to your Gmail. Disconnect Gmail or your phone separately any time.

Questions or complaints

Write to __CONTACT__ (our grievance contact under India's DPDP Act), or reply to any email from Kitna. We answer data requests as quickly as we can and always within the time India's data protection rules allow.

Not financial advice

Kitna helps you budget and save. It does not give investment, tax or legal advice. See also the terms of use.
